# App Store Screenshot Copy

Copy for the App Store screenshot deck. Positions 1–2 are fixed (Start, Statistics); the rest follow a **progressive zoom-out then differentiator finish**:

**start → last night → week → month → hear it → act on it.**

Each screen lists a **primary** recommendation plus **alternatives** (different angle or shorter length). Primary titles open with distinct verbs/framings so nothing repeats.

---

## 1. `Start Recording Screen first screen.png`

**Primary**
- **Title:** Sleep Better, Starting Tonight
- **Subtitle:** Set your phone down, hit record, and let SleepGuard listen while you sleep.

**Alternatives**
| Title | Subtitle |
|-------|----------|
| One Tap to a Better Night | Just set your phone on the nightstand — SleepGuard does the rest. |
| Your Bedside Sleep Lab | Start a session and monitor your night, all on your device. |
| Press Record. Sleep. | Effortless nightly tracking that runs quietly in the background. |

*Why:* The opening frame sells the core promise and the effortless ritual. "Starting Tonight" creates immediacy; the subtitle mirrors the on-screen action while reinforcing the hands-off simplicity.

## Deck cohesion

Primary titles open with distinct verbs/framings so nothing repeats:

> Sleep Better · Wake Up · Watch · Your Whole Story · Hear · Quiet

Arc: **start → last night → week → month → hear it → act on it.** Positions 2–4 keep the chart-heavy analytics together (progressive zoom-out); positions 5–6 finish on the emotional differentiators (proof, then action). All copy is short enough to sit comfortably above a device mockup in Apple's clean marketing style.

**Alternate ordering to consider:** if audio playback tests as your top converter, promote `snoring session summary details.png` to position 3 and run the analytics as 4–5 — at the cost of splitting the two chart-heavy screens.
